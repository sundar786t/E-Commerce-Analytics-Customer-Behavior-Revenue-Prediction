# 🛒 E-Commerce Analytics: Customer Behavior & Revenue Prediction  

This project focuses on analyzing **customer behavior in e-commerce platforms** and building predictive models to estimate **revenue**.  
It provides insights into buying patterns, customer segmentation, and factors that drive sales, helping businesses optimize marketing and improve customer retention.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn)  
- **Visualization**: Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly  
- **Machine Learning**: Regression, Classification, XGBoost  
- **Jupyter Notebook** for exploration  

---

## 📊 Workflow  
1. **Data Preprocessing** – clean data, handle missing values, encode categorical variables  
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – customer demographics, product preferences, purchase frequency  
3. **Feature Engineering** – RFM metrics, customer segments, revenue drivers  
4. **Model Training** – regression & ML algorithms for revenue prediction  
5. **Evaluation** – metrics like RMSE, R², accuracy, confusion matrix  
6. **Insights** – identify key customer segments & revenue patterns  

---

## 📈 Results & Insights  
- Discovered customer groups with **higher purchase frequency & revenue contribution**  
- Built a model to **predict customer revenue** with good accuracy  
- Visualizations reveal **trends in sales & customer behavior**
